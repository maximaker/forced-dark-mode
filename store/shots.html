Dark mode for sites
that don't have one.

Turn it on per site. It follows the sun: neutral by day, warmer and dimmer after sunset.

  • One switch per site, or Alt+Shift+D
  • Fades to a warm evening palette at sunset
  • Optional bedtime mode for late reading
